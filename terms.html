About our fragrances

This is the part we want read carefully, because it is the one people get wrong.

They are our own
Every fragrance we sell is blended and bottled as Shafaaf Perfumes. Some are inspired by well-known scent families — oud, musk, gourmand, floral — the way any perfumer works within a tradition.
What they are not
They are not copies, replicas or duplicates of any designer perfume, and we never describe them that way. We are not connected with, approved by, or acting for any other brand. Where we mention a well-known fragrance, it is only to describe how ours smells — the name stays the property of whoever owns it.
Scent is personal
How long a fragrance lasts and how it smells on you depends on your skin, the weather and how much you use. We describe our blends honestly; we cannot promise they will behave the same on every person.

Prices
All prices are in Indian Rupees and include tax. We may change a price at any time, but never after you have paid for an order.

When the payment goes through and you get our confirmation. Until then it is a request, not a sale.

If something sells out or a price was shown wrongly, we may cancel the order and return your money in full. We will tell you why.
Delivery and returns

Using the products, and the site

Use with care
Perfume and attar are for external use on skin or clothing. Try a little first if your skin is sensitive, keep it away from eyes, flame and small children, and store it out of direct sun. Bakhoor is burnt on charcoal — never leave it unattended.
