Our Story

Fragrance, made honest.

Shafaaf Perfumes is a premium perfume brand offering high quality, long-lasting, internationally inspired fragrances for men, women and unisex wear — crafted with care and priced without the usual luxury markup.

Why We Started

A fragrance house built on transparency

Too many "premium" fragrances are either genuinely excellent and priced out of reach, or affordable and forgettable. Shafaaf Perfumes exists in between — real oils, real longevity, and prices that make sense.

Every one of our fourteen fragrances is offered as both a Perfume and a concentrated Attar, so you can choose the ritual that fits you: a confident daily spray, or a long-wearing oil applied close to the skin.
