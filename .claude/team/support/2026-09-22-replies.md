# WhatsApp Reply Templates — 2026-09-22

Paste-ready. Fill in `{Name}`, `{Product}`, `{Order}` (and `[Product]` where you're
naming a specific fragrance) before sending. Lines marked `[OWNER CONFIRM: …]`
are not settled yet — see the report for the full list of questions. Every
template stays under 60 words and never promises a date, discount, refund or
policy the brand hasn't confirmed.

---

## Sales

### 1. Price / size question
**English:** Hi {Name}! [Product] comes in Perfume (30ml ₹599 / 50ml ₹899) and Attar (6ml ₹349 / 12ml ₹699). Free shipping across India, pay easily via UPI/card on our site shafaafperfumes.com. Want help picking a size?
**Hinglish:** Hi {Name}! [Product] Perfume mein 30ml ₹599 / 50ml ₹899 aur Attar mein 6ml ₹349 / 12ml ₹699 mein milta hai. Poore India mein free shipping, site shafaafperfumes.com par UPI/card se pay kar sakte ho. Size choose karne mein madad karun?

### 2. "Which one should I buy"
**English:** Great question, {Name}! Tell me what you like — fresh & light, sweet & warm, or deep oud/woody? I'll suggest 2–3 from our 14 fragrances (Oud, Musk, Floral, Gourmand, Fresh, Woody, Spicy). Bestsellers: Oud Kaaba, Khamrah Spl, Ameer Al Oud Gold.
**Hinglish:** Achha sawal, {Name}! Bataiye aapko kaisa scent pasand hai — fresh & light, sweet & warm, ya deep oud/woody? Main 14 fragrances mein se 2-3 suggest karta hoon. Bestsellers: Oud Kaaba, Khamrah Spl, Ameer Al Oud Gold.

## Problems

### 13. Late parcel

**Hinglish:** Delay ke liye maafi, {Name}. Order {Order} abhi check karta hoon. [OWNER CONFIRM: delay ki wajah/naya estimate] — courier se pata chalte hi update karunga.

### 14. Wrong/damaged item
**English:** So sorry to hear that, {Name}. Could you share a photo of {Product} and order number {Order}? [OWNER CONFIRM: replacement or refund policy] — we'll sort this out for you.
**Hinglish:** Ye sunke bura laga, {Name}. Kya aap {Product} aur order {Order} ki photo bhej sakte hain? [OWNER CONFIRM: replacement ya refund policy] — hum ise theek karenge.

### 15. "The smell is not like the original"
**English:** Thanks for the feedback, {Name}. Just a reminder — {Product} is our own blend inspired by the fragrance, not a copy of it, so some difference is expected. Would you like suggestions for a closer match from our range?
**Hinglish:** Feedback ke liye shukriya, {Name}. Bas yaad dila dun — {Product} humara apna blend hai us fragrance se inspired, uski copy nahi, isliye thoda farak ho sakta hai. Hamari range se closer match suggest karun?

### 16. Refund question
**English:** Understood, {Name}. [OWNER CONFIRM: refund/returns policy and timeline] for order {Order} — let me confirm the details and get back to you shortly.
**Hinglish:** Samajh gaya, {Name}. Order {Order} ke liye [OWNER CONFIRM: refund/returns policy aur timeline] — details confirm karke jaldi reply karta hoon.

### 17. Rude message
**English:** I hear you, {Name}, and I'm sorry for the frustration. Let's sort this out — could you share your order number {Order} so I can look into it properly?
**Hinglish:** Samajh raha hoon, {Name}, aur is takleef ke liye maafi. Chaliye ise theek karte hain — order number {Order} share kar sakte hain taaki main dhang se dekh sakun?
