## 3. Three blog/guide page proposals

### Proposal 1 — "Attar vs Perfume: What's the Real Difference (and Which Should You Choose)?"
- **Target keyword:** attar vs perfume difference / how long does attar last
- **Why it can rank:** every one of Shafaaf's 14 fragrances is sold in both forms, so this question is asked by real buyers on-site already (product.js accordion, line 162, already answers a shortened version: "Perfume vs. Attar — Which Should I Choose?"). It is a genuine, currently-unclaimed content gap — expanding that existing accordion answer into a full guide reuses copy the owner has already approved in spirit.
- **H2 outline:**
  - What is an attar? (oil-based concentrate, no alcohol)
  - What is a perfume/EDP spray?
  - Longevity and projection compared
  - Which is better for hot Indian summers / for travel / for religious settings (alcohol-free attar note)
  - How to apply each correctly
  - Shafaaf's take: every fragrance in both forms
- **Products to link:** 2-3 fragrances available in both forms with strong reviews, e.g. Oud Kaaba, Ameer Al Oud Gold, Shanaya Gold — link both the Perfume and Attar variant.

### Proposal 2 — "How to Burn Bakhoor at Home: A Beginner's Guide"
- **Target keyword:** how to burn bakhoor at home / bakhoor online india
- **Why it can rank:** bakhoor is the newest, least-explained category on the site (shop.html shows a "coming soon" state per shop.js line 185-192 for sections with no stock yet — this guide gives Google something to index for "bakhoor" queries even while some bakhoor SKUs are still filling in) and it's a lower-competition keyword than "perfume"/"attar" generally.
- **H2 outline:**
  - What is bakhoor and where does the tradition come from
  - What you need: charcoal disc / electric burner, bakhoor warmer
  - Step-by-step: lighting charcoal safely, placing the bakhoor chips
  - How long a session should last, ventilation tips
  - Bakhoor vs. incense sticks vs. attar — when to use which
  - Shafaaf's 5 bakhoor scents compared
- **Products to link:** all 5 bakhoor — Amber Oud, Al Kaaf, Al Noor, Amir Al Oud, Khamrah.

### Proposal 3 — "Best Oud Fragrances in India: A Buyer's Guide to Shafaaf's Oud Collection"
- **Target keyword:** best oud fragrance india / oud attar online
- **Why it can rank:** Oud is the single biggest family in the catalog (Ameer Al Oud Gold, White Oud Spl, Oud Magestic, Oud Kaaba, plus Amber Oud and Amir Al Oud bakhoor — 6 of 19 SKUs) and "oud" is consistently one of the highest-intent search terms in the Indian fragrance category; a comparison guide lets all of them rank together instead of competing with each other for the same head term.
- **H2 outline:**
  - What makes oud different from other fragrance notes
  - Shafaaf's oud lineup at a glance (perfume, attar, and bakhoor oud options)
  - Ameer Al Oud Gold vs. Oud Kaaba vs. Oud Magestic vs. White Oud Spl — how they differ
  - Oud for daily wear vs. special occasions
  - Pairing an oud attar with an oud bakhoor
- **Products to link:** Ameer Al Oud Gold, Oud Kaaba, Oud Magestic, White Oud Spl, Amber Oud bakhoor, Amir Al Oud bakhoor.

*(All three guides need an actual page/route to live on — currently there is no blog/guide template in the codebase. That is a separate small build task, not something this audit can spec further without the owner deciding where guides should live, e.g. `/guides/attar-vs-perfume`.)*

## 4. What to redo when the real domain arrives

- Every `<link rel="canonical">` proposed above (and any added later) must be updated from `https://shafaaf-perfumes.shafaafperfumes.workers.dev/...` to the real domain.
- Every Open Graph/Twitter `og:url` and `og:image` (proposed in §1) needs the real domain.
- Any JSON-LD `url`/`logo`/`image` fields need the real domain.
- Re-submit `/sitemap.xml` under the new domain in Google Search Console **and** add the new domain as its own GSC property (a domain change is not automatic — GSC treats `workers.dev` and the new domain as different properties). Use GSC's "Change of Address" tool if available for the domain type in use.
- `worker.js` itself needs **no code change** — it already builds every URL from `url.origin` (confirmed at lines 14-15, 39-56), so sitemap/robots automatically pick up the new domain the moment DNS points there.
- Update the Instagram/Facebook/YouTube bio links and the WhatsApp catalog (if one exists) to the new domain.
- Re-check that HTTPS redirect / any old `workers.dev` links floating around (shared on WhatsApp, printed materials) 301-redirect to the new domain rather than 404ing, so link equity isn't lost.
