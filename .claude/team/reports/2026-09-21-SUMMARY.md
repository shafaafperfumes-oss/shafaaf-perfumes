## 1. Is hafte ka nichod

- **Sabse bada trust issue:** product ke "reviews" aur "ratings" (jaise 4.7 stars, 214 reviews) **fake hain** — code khud inhe generate karta hai, aur kuch reviewer ke sheher Pakistan ke hain (Lahore, Karachi) jabki business India-based hai. Newsletter form bhi "Thanks for subscribing" dikhata hai lekin email kahin save nahi hota. Dono cheezein customer ka bharosa tod sakti hain.
- WhatsApp ya Facebook par jab koi product link share karta hai, preview mein sirf generic "Fragrance — Shafaaf Perfumes" dikhta hai — sahi product naam ya photo nahi aata, kyunki title/description sirf browser mein JavaScript chalne ke baad set hoti hai. WhatsApp hi aapka main order channel hai, isliye ye bada nuksaan hai.
- Poore site par "canonical tag" aur social-share (Open Graph) tags kahin nahi hain, aur Google Search Console/Analytics bhi set up nahi hai — matlab abhi track nahi ho raha ki log site par kya dhoondh kar aa rahe hain.
- Checkout mein guest checkout nahi hai — order dene se pehle account banana zaroori hai, jo bahut logo ko cart chhod dene ki wajah banta hai.
- Baaki cheezein theek hain: sitemap.xml/robots.txt sahi kaam kar rahe hain, koi broken link nahi mila. Sirf logo image bada hai (337KB) aur 5 product descriptions chhoti thi — dono ke liye ready-made sujhaav is report mein hain, approval chahiye.
