Our Story

Crafted for the modern fragrance lover

Shafaaf Perfumes was founded on a simple idea: premium, long-lasting, internationally inspired fragrance shouldn't require an international price tag. Every fragrance is offered in both Perfume and concentrated Attar form, so you can choose the strength and ritual that suits you.

Every bottle is crafted with care, tested for longevity, and shipped with the kind of attention you'd expect from a fragrance house many times our size.
